### Do these three things first

**1. Change your password.**
Click your name at the bottom-left → **Change password**. Pick something only
you know. Nobody else can see it, including me.

**2. Fill in your profile.**
Left menu → **My Profile**. Your details, bank account for payments, and — most
importantly — your **emergency contact**. You'll be on live event sites; if
something happens I need to be able to reach someone. Please don't skip it.

**3. File your first daily report.**
On your **Command Center** you'll see a prompt at the top. Click **File report**,
write what you did, and submit. It takes about thirty seconds.

---

### The one habit that matters

**File a daily report at the end of every working day — Monday, Wednesday and
Thursday.** Three a week.

It asks three things: what you did, what's next, and anything blocking you.
That third box is the one I read first. If something is stuck, say so there —
that's how I know to unblock it rather than finding out a week later.

Everything else we track is built on this. If the reports stop, nothing else
in the system means anything.

---

### What's in your workspace

**Command Center** — your home. Your tasks, your weekly objective, your report
prompt. Start here every day.

**Projects & Events** — the jobs we're delivering. You'll see project names,
dates, venues, crew and deliverables.

**Vendors** — our suppliers. You can add a new vendor and attach vendors to a
project with what they're providing and what they cost. Anything you add comes
to me for approval before it counts, so don't worry about getting it wrong.

**CRM & Sales** — our clients and enquiries. There's a one-line box at the top:
type a name, press Enter, done. Use it for anyone who contacts us or anyone
we're chasing.

**SOP & Knowledge** — how we do things. Read the **Vendor Engagement SOP**,
**Basic Procurement Policy** and **Standard Event Checklist** in your first
week. They answer most questions before you have to ask me.

**Messages** — message me directly in the app. Use it for anything that isn't
urgent; WhatsApp for anything that is.

**My Profile** — your employment record.

---

### Already waiting for you

**Your weekly objective:** *Register Digit One Zero on one government vendor
portal.* Open Command Center and you'll see it.

Government work is awarded through prequalification — if we're not registered,
we can't be awarded, no matter how good the work is. That's why this is first.

**Your learning plan** is on your Command Center too, laid out quarter by
quarter for the twelve months.

---

### What you won't see, and why

You won't see project budgets, revenue, profit or margins anywhere. That isn't
about trust — it's how the system is built, and it's the same for everyone
except me. You'll see what a vendor charges on a job you're coordinating,
because you can't coordinate it otherwise.

---

### Your week

**Monday** — Operations. Update the system with last week's work, agree
priorities with me.
**Wednesday** — Market access. Vendor portals, registrations, tenders.
**Thursday** — Documentation, your weekly report, and our mentoring session.

Thursday is the anchor. Even if Monday gets eaten by an event, the week closes
properly.

---

Anything that doesn't make sense, message me in the app or ask on Thursday.
Nothing here is a test — if something is confusing, that's worth telling me,
because it's probably confusing for everyone.

Welcome aboard.
