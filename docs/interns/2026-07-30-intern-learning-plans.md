## Read this first: the load is real

Both job descriptions were written as if for a full-time role. On 3 days a week
that is **~144 working days across the whole internship**, not 250.

Esther's stated monthly deliverables total roughly **23 content pieces** (12 posts,
4 videos, 1 case study, 1 blog, 4 founder LinkedIn posts, 1 thought-leadership
article) across ~12 working days — about **two finished pieces per working day**,
on top of shooting, editing and portfolio work.

That is achievable **only with batching** — one day producing, one day publishing —
and only if "12 social posts" means posts assembled from footage already shot on
project days, not 12 original shoots. It is not achievable as 12 separate creative
projects. The plans below are built around batching for exactly this reason.

Arome's load (20 companies researched, 10 qualified prospects, 1 SOP, 1 workflow,
1 checklist monthly) is realistic on 3 days — research batches well.

**Recommendation:** run Month 1 as written, then compare actual output against
target at the first monthly review and adjust the numbers rather than letting
them quietly slip. A target nobody hits stops being a target.

## The shared weekly rhythm

Each day has one primary purpose. Context-switching is the enemy of a 3-day week.

| Day | Arome — Operations & Growth | Esther — Brand & Communications |
|---|---|---|
| **Monday** | Operations day: weekly priorities, project tracker, ops meeting, task follow-ups | Planning & writing day: content calendar, captions, article drafts |
| **Wednesday** | Business development day: prospect research, CRM updates, follow-ups | Production day: graphics, video editing, shooting, BTS capture |
| **Thursday** | Documentation day: SOPs, proposals, **weekly report**, mentoring session | Publishing day: schedule posts, portfolio/website updates, **weekly report**, mentoring |

**Both file a daily report at the end of every working day** — DOZ OS → Field Mode
→ File Daily Report. Three per week, twelve per month. This is non-negotiable and
is the single easiest KPI to measure.

**Thursday is the anchor day.** Weekly report + mentoring both land there, so the
week always closes properly even if Monday or Wednesday gets disrupted by an event.

## What the app supports today, and what it doesn't

**Works now:**
- Daily reports (Field Mode) — both interns, three times a week
- Weekly reports
- Task assignment from Staff Hub
- Content calendar (Marketing module) — **but only FOUNDER and STAFF can reach it**
- CRM client and enquiry entry — **also FOUNDER/STAFF only**
- Messages — founder ↔ intern, both directions
- My Profile — employment records

**⚠️ Blocking gap:** `INTERN` role grants only Command Center, Field Mode, SOP,
Help, My Profile and Messages. **Neither intern can reach Marketing or CRM** —
which is where most of their actual deliverables live. Arome cannot add a client
or log a prospect; Esther cannot touch the content calendar.

**Fix:** use the per-user permissions override (Team → Edit member → Permissions).
Suggested grants:
- **Arome:** Command Center, Field Mode, **CRM & Sales**, **Projects**, SOP, Help, My Profile, Messages
- **Esther:** Command Center, Field Mode, **Marketing & Growth**, SOP, Help, My Profile, Messages

Neither needs Finance, Procurement or Team.

**Not built yet, needed for these plans:**
- SOP creation (module is read-only — Arome cannot write the SOP his KPI requires)
- Case studies / portfolio tracking (Phase 2 of the contracted-revenue spec)
- Course and learning-session tracking
- A visible daily-report prompt on Command Center — the feature exists but is
  buried in a module called "Field Mode", so nobody finds it
